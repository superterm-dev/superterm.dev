Is that word right? Press `^G w` in the TUI — or `Cmd+Alt+S` on the
web — and check it as you type. A built-in British English dictionary
answers in under a millisecond, offline, with a red-line on the input
and ranked suggestions. Press **1–5** to accept one and `Enter` to
drop it straight into your session.

For jargon the dictionary has never met, a local language model is the
second line — consulted only when the dictionary admits a blank, and
never required: with no model configured, the dictionary is the whole
answer.
