Attach a long PDF, or link a long page, and ask what you need from it. A
separate model reads every page in a fresh context and hands back only the
answer, with page citations, so the conversation stays small.

In the clip, GLM-5.3 Flash studies NIST SP 800-207, all 59 pages, and the
chat holds 8k tokens afterwards. The wait is sped up and labelled.
