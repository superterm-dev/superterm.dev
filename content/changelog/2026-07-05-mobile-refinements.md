title: Mobile — swipe, zoom, and attach
---

Swipe in from the left edge to reveal the session picker, and swipe it
away again. New **A-/A+** buttons in the header nudge the terminal text
size and remember your choice, and a paperclip beside the microphone
hands photos, text files, and PDFs straight to an agent (Pro).

Dictation capture was rebuilt for phones too: audio is processed off
the main thread so recording never fights terminal rendering, streaming
uses a third of the bandwidth, and your first words appear in under a
second. Overview tiles no longer collapse to empty bars on small
screens.
