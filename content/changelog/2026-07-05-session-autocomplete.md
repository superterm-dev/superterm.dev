title: Session names that remember themselves
media: /media/session-autocomplete
---

Typing a new session name now offers a proper combobox: a
keyboard-navigable list under the field with the typed portion
highlighted, prefix matches first. Names come from superterm's
all-time record, so long-gone sessions are recalled on every device —
handy when your names come in families like `inlets-pro` and
`inlets-uplink`.

Picking a name that's already running switches to that session instead
of creating a duplicate.
