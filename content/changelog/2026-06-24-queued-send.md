title: Queued send — agents pick back up on your schedule
---

Out of credit until your top-up lands in a few hours? Queue the message
now and superterm sends it at the time you set, so the agent carries on
the moment it can — no babysitting, no missed window.

Sends are smoothed out to avoid the flicker you'd otherwise see when
keys are replayed into a session.
