title: Paste and upload images straight to your agent
---

Drop a screenshot, mockup, or error dialog right into the composer — no
more describing what's on screen. Works with any agent that accepts
image input.

You can also drag an image from anywhere on your desktop straight onto
the terminal — a drop zone appears, and the file lands where the agent
can read it.
