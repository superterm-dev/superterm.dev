Ask for a chart and the chat draws one in place: bars, lines, or pie, from
your numbers or any table it writes. Charts come along when you export the
chat.

In the clip, the numbers are our own warm voice-stack timings on an RTX 5060
Ti and a DGX Spark, charted by GLM-5.3 Flash. Nothing is sped up.
