title: Sort the overview by what needs you
---

The overview now ranks **Activity** properly — running state first,
then live CPU, then output — so a busy session surfaces instead of
sinking into an alphabetical list. **Idle** flips to show the most
recently active session first, and **Starred** keeps your favourites
on top.

Pick a mode from the header (it's remembered), and the order freezes
while you look — tiles rank meaningfully without jumping around under
you.
