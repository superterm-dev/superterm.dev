title: Copy and paste that works across browsers — and inside TUIs
---

Switch machine or agent and copy/paste usually breaks. Not any more.
Selection now copies cleanly in **every browser**, and you can grab the
terminal selection as **Markdown, HTML, or plain text**.

It even works in and out of full-screen TUIs like **opencode** and
**amp**, where the terminal normally swallows your selection. Multi-line
pastes no longer get mangled by editors, and `Esc` is snappy in TUIs.
